About Dwarf Names in D&D 5e

In the Player's Handbook (p.20), dwarves live about 350 years and belong to one of two subraces: hill dwarf or mountain dwarf. A dwarf's name consists of a given name plus a clan name — and the clan name matters more. The PHB notes that a dwarf's name is not his own; it belongs to his clan, and a dwarf's loyalty to clan exceeds even their own life.

Sample given names from the PHB table:

  • Male: Baern, Bruenor, Dain, Flint, Harbek, Thorin, Tordek
  • Female: Audhild, Eldeth, Helga, Kathra

Dwarf Clan Names

Clan names in the PHB are drawn from ancestors' deeds, mines, strongholds, and battlefields. Sample clan names from the PHB: Battlehammer, Fireforge, Frostbeard, Ironfist.

Bruenor Battlehammer — a well-known dwarf from R.A. Salvatore's The Crystal Shard — is a good example of the given + clan structure in published D&D fiction.

Choosing a Name for Different Dwarf Characters

  • Warrior: A short, hard name that is easy to call out in combat — Baern Flint Harbek.
  • Blacksmith: Pair a given name with a clan name tied to metalworking — Fireforge Ironfist.
  • Miner or explorer: Clan names connected to stone and strongholds fit well — Frostbeard Battlehammer.
  • Clan leader: Use the full given + clan name formally — Bruenor Battlehammer Dain Ironfist.

Tips for Creating a Dwarf Name

  • Start with a given name that is easy to pronounce. Dwarf names in the PHB are short and use firm consonants.
  • Add a clan name if your setting uses clan identity — the PHB treats clan as central to dwarf culture.
  • Keep names distinct within a party; the PHB tables have enough variety to avoid duplicates.
  • Choose a name that works spoken aloud at the table.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are some official D&D 5e dwarf names?

The Player's Handbook (p.20) lists dwarf given names including Baern, Bruenor, Dain, Flint, Harbek, Thorin, and Tordek for males, and Audhild, Eldeth, Helga, and Kathra for females. Clan names include Battlehammer, Fireforge, Frostbeard, and Ironfist.

Do dwarf characters need surnames?

In the PHB, clan names are central to dwarf identity — a dwarf's name belongs to the clan. For a simple one-shot character a given name may suffice, but for a character with backstory, the clan name establishes ancestry and social standing.

What's the difference between hill dwarf and mountain dwarf names?

Both use the same given + clan name structure from the PHB. The subrace choice affects ability scores and traits more than naming convention, though mountain dwarves are often associated with stronghold-based clan names.

Can I use these names in my D&D campaign?
