Contact Us

Get in Touch

We'd love to hear from you! Whether you have feedback, suggestions, questions, or just want to share how you've used our tools, please don't hesitate to reach out.

Get in Touch

The best way to contact us is by email:

Email: contact@fantasyname.net

We typically respond to all inquiries within 24-48 hours.

What to Include

When contacting us, please include:

  • Your name (optional)
  • Your email address (for response)
  • Subject line indicating the nature of your inquiry
  • Detailed message with any relevant information

Types of Inquiries

We welcome all types of feedback and inquiries, including:

  • Bug Reports: Found an issue with our generators? Let us know!
  • Feature Requests: Have an idea for a new generator or feature? We're all ears.
  • Content Suggestions: Want us to write about a specific naming convention or race?
  • General Feedback: Tell us what you like and what we can improve.
  • Partnership Inquiries: Interested in collaborating with us?
  • Press Inquiries: Media requests should be directed to our press email.

Community

Join our community of fantasy naming enthusiasts:

  • Share your generated names on social media with #FantasyNameGenerator
  • Tag us in your RPG campaigns, stories, or games that use our names
  • Follow us for updates on new generators and features

Support Our Work

If you find our tools useful, there are several ways you can support us:

  • Spread the Word: Tell your friends, gaming groups, and writing communities about our generators
  • Provide Feedback: Help us improve by sharing your thoughts and suggestions
  • Report Issues: Let us know if you encounter any bugs or problems
  • Link to Us: If you have a website or blog, consider linking to our tools

FAQ

Before contacting us, you might find answers to common questions in our guides section or on our about page.

Response Time

We strive to respond to all inquiries as quickly as possible. Typical response times:

  • Bug Reports: 24 hours
  • Feature Requests: 48 hours
  • General Inquiries: 48-72 hours
  • Partnership Inquiries: 1 week

Thank You

Thank you for using Fantasy Name Generator! Your support and feedback help us create better tools for the fantasy community. We look forward to hearing from you.
