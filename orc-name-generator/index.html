About Orc Names

This page covers two related but distinct needs:

  1. Half-Orc character names — names for player characters of the Half-Orc race.
  2. Orc names — names for NPC orcs, tribal leaders, or fantasy world-building.

The Player's Handbook (p.40-41) describes the Half-Orc race and includes sample names. Half-Orcs may use orc-style names or human names depending on which culture they were raised in. For orc culture and naming traditions beyond the PHB, Volo's Guide to Monsters (2016) provides expanded lore on orc tribes, religion, and social structure.

Half-Orc Names from the Player's Handbook

Sample Half-Orc names from the PHB (p.41):

  • Male: Ardn, Bagruk, Rendarth, Surt, Trag
  • Female: Baggi, Emen, Eveng, Gaarlag, Nulb

The PHB notes that half-orcs who grow up among humans may use human names instead. Those raised among orcs use orc-style names like those above.

Orc Naming Traditions

Volo's Guide to Monsters (2016) expands on orc culture beyond the PHB, covering tribal structure, religion, and warfare. Orc society is organized around tribes, and an orc's tribal identity is central to their place in the world. The Monster Manual describes orcs as aggressive raiders who worship Gruumsh, the one-eyed god of war.

For naming, orcs in published D&D material use short, harsh-sounding names. There is no official orcish name table in the core rules — DMs typically create orc names that fit the guttural, martial character described in these sources.

Gruumsh and Orc Culture

Gruumsh is the patron deity of orcs, described in both the Monster Manual and Volo's Guide to Monsters. He is a one-eyed god of war and conquest. Orc culture, as described in Volo's Guide, centers on tribal warfare, strength, and devotion to Gruumsh. These values influence naming — orc names and epithets tend to reflect martial accomplishment rather than craft or scholarship.

Half-Orc Characters: Choosing a Name

  • Raised among orcs: Use an orc-style name from the PHB table. Add a tribe name if your setting uses tribal identity.
  • Raised among humans: Use a human name from the PHB human name tables. The half-orc's orc heritage may still show in appearance or personality.
  • Between cultures: Some half-orcs use a human given name with an orc tribe name, reflecting their mixed heritage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are there official D&D 5e orc name tables?

The Player's Handbook (p.41) includes sample Half-Orc names. Volo's Guide to Monsters (2016) provides expanded orc culture and naming lore but does not include a separate full orc name table.

What's the difference between a half-orc name and an orc name?

Half-Orcs are a player race (PHB p.40). They may use orc-style names or human names depending on upbringing. Orc names belong to full orcs — tribal creatures described in the Monster Manual and Volo's Guide to Monsters. This generator covers both.

Do half-orc characters need a tribe or clan name?

Not always. The PHB focuses on personal names for half-orcs. If your setting includes orc tribal structure (as described in Volo's Guide), a tribe name adds context, but a personal name alone is sufficient for most characters.

Can I use these names in my D&D campaign?

Names generated by this tool are original combinations. Names drawn from the PHB are official D&D content and are fine for home campaigns. For published or commercial work, check the Open Gaming License and System Reference Document to confirm what content is licensed for reuse.
