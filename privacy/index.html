Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience on our website. Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help us remember your preferences and understand how you use our site.

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and preferences

Managing Cookies

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of our website.

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services that collect, monitor, and analyze data to improve our website's functionality. These third-party service providers have their own privacy policies addressing how they use information.

Privacy

This site is a set of static HTML files with client-side JavaScript. When you click Generate, the JS reads a JSON corpus in memory, assembles a name, and paints it to the DOM. There is no server-side component to send data to.

No account. No database. No analytics on generated names. Your browser's speech synthesis API is called only when you click the speaker icon. Reload the page and the session is gone.

The optional LLM-enhanced mode (if enabled) calls the API endpoint you configure, with your own key stored in localStorage. Your key never leaves your browser.
